About Us

Alchemy Logic Consulting is a leading IT and business services consultancy headquartered in New Jersey, USA. We enable business transformation for our clients through the innovative use of technology.

We work closely with our clients to unlock their potential -- enabling change that increases efficiency, accelerates growth, and mitigates risk. We leverage our in-depth industry knowledge, technical expertise, and global delivery capabilities to help our customers establish leadership positions in their markets.

Alchemy Logic Consulting Limited was established in 2011 as a leader in providing end-to-end solutions for information technology and telecommunications infrastructure. We are well-positioned with resources and experience to provide world-class IT solutions to corporate and governmental institutions in Nigeria.

Some of the clients for whom the firm has carried out assignments include universities, banks, educational institutions, telecommunications companies, government parastatals and establishments, as well as companies in the oil and gas, manufacturing, and commercial sectors.

The Alchemy Logic Consulting Way

At Alchemy Logic Consulting, our cultural journey began with defining values that reflect a shared, lived experience for our employees, clients, partners, and stakeholders. The Alchemy Logic Consulting Way was created to ensure every interaction delivers mutual benefit and lasting success.

Our approach emerged from a global culture exchange between leaders and employees, built on trust, empathy for diverse perspectives, and complete transparency. It's more than a framework --- it's the way we collaborate, innovate, and deliver value every day.
